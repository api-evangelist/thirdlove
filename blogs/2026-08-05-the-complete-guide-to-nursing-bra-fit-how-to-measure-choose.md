Key Takeaways A well-fitting nursing bra gets most of its support from a level, snug band, with cups that fully encase the breast and a center panel lying flat. Measure your underbust for band size and the fullest part of your bust for cup size while your breasts are full, then subtract to find your starting size. Expect to size up in both band and cup while breastfeeding, and leave adjustment room for daily fullness changes.
