Key Takeaways Underwear styles differ in two ways that matter for fit: rise, or where the waistband sits, and coverage, or how much fabric covers your back. Match the cut to your curves, like high-rise briefs if you're an apple shape or a wider leg opening if you're a pear. Your size comes from your hip measurement, and since a too-small size causes most rolling and digging, use Thirdlove's XS-3X range to size right.
