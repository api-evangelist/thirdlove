Chef and Creator Sophia Roe ( @sophia_roe ) spends her days close to the source — in conversation with the farmers and makers who grow her ingredients, then behind the stove turning their work into something worth sharing. It's a craft built on respect: for the land, for the people who tend it, and for the process that can't be rushed. "My foundation is food straight from the source and comfort that feels effortless." This is what Style Foundations means when it talks about effortlessness — not a big gesture, but the first layer that goes on before anything else does, quietly setting the tone
