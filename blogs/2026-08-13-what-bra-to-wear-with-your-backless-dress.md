Key Takeaways An adhesive backless bra is the most invisible bra for a backless dress, leaving your entire back bare under low and plunging cuts. Strapless and convertible bras are the better pick for higher or less-revealing backs, and convert to halter or criss-cross setups. Nipple covers (paired with breast tape when you want lift) are the go-braless solution for the most barely-there, sheer, or minimal-coverage looks.
