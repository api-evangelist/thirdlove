Key Takeaways You can show lace with a sheer blouse, a low-cut wrap top, a blazer worn without a shirt, or an open-back knit. Show just a hint of lace at an office party or family dinner, and save fully visible lace for New Year's Eve and date night. Black lace is your most versatile color, red reads instantly festive, and a neutral close to your skin tone keeps contrast low.
