Key Takeaways Aging softens breast tissue and shifts volume, posture, and skin sensitivity, so the bras that once fit often need to change too. Prioritize support first, then comfort features like wide cushioned straps, wide smoothing bands, soft breathable fabrics, full coverage, and easy closures. Wireless bras are the most comfortable choice for most women as they age, while a well-fitted underwire still suits those wanting extra structure.
