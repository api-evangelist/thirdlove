Key Takeaways Breasts sag due to age, pregnancy and breastfeeding, weight changes, genetics, and gravity, and the right bra restores lift and support. The most supportive bras for sagging breasts pair a wide, firm band with molded or memory-foam cups, plus side and back smoothing. Full-coverage, underwire, minimizer, and push-up styles each help in different ways, from even containment to targeted lift.
