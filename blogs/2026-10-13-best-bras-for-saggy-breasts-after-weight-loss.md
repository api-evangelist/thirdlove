Key Takeaways Breasts often sag after weight loss because they lose fat volume, especially at the top, and stretched skin may not fully bounce back. A full-coverage or T-shirt bra with structured or memory-foam cups and a snug band is usually the best choice for a relaxed shape. Wireless bras can lift deflated breasts when they pair a firm band with shaped cups, while push-ups work best as an occasional option.
