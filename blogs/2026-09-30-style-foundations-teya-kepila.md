Teya Kepila is a Brooklyn-based ceramic artist and ceremony host, the maker behind The Turmeric Times - a practice built around functional ceramics, community, and the small rituals that make up a more intentional life. Her work moves slowly and deliberately, shaped as much by journaling and travel as it is by clay. "My foundation is the openness to feel inspired by all of life's offerings." This is what Style Foundations means when it talks about a reset - not a big gesture, but the first layer that goes on before anything else does, quietly setting the tone for everything that follows.
