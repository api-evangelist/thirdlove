Key Takeaways Bra sister sizes share the same cup volume with a different band size, so a 34C equals a 32D or a 36B. Sister size up when your band is tight on the loosest hook, and sister size down when it's loose on the tightest hook. If your band fits but your cups don't, change only your cup size instead of switching to a sister size.
