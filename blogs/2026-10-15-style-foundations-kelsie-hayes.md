Kelsie Hayes is a visionary florist, creative director, entrepreneur, and author. For years, she has brought beauty into borrowed spaces, transforming storefronts, showrooms, hotels, restaurants, and blank slates into environments filled with feeling. HOUSE OF THREE is the next evolution: a permanent home for her creative point of view and a platform for the community she's built.
