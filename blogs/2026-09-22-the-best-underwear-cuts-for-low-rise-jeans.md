Key Takeaways The best underwear for low-rise jeans is a thong, cheeky, hipster, or low-rise bikini with a waistband at or below your hip bones. Skip high briefs, full-coverage briefs, wide-band boyshorts, and thick elastic waistbands, since they sit too high or add bulk under denim. Compare your jeans' rise to your underwear's band, then sit, bend, and squat in front of a mirror to check for peeking.
