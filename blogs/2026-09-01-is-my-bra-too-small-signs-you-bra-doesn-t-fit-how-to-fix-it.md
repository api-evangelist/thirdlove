Key Takeaways Spillover, a lifting center gore, wire pressure on breast tissue, and deep band marks are the clearest signs your bra is too small. Pain around your ribcage points to a tight band, while spillover or wire pressure on your breasts points to a small cup. Go up a cup for tight cups, try your sister size for a tight band, or pick a half cup to land between sizes.
