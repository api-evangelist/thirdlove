This October marks five years of Thirdlove's partnership with Living Beyond Breast Cancer ( LBBC ), the national nonprofit that gives people affected by breast cancer the trusted information, connection, and support to make their own decisions about their care. Five years in, it's a partnership built on a simple belief: no one should have to navigate a diagnosis alone. Health advocacy isn't a solo journey - it's how we show up for each other.
