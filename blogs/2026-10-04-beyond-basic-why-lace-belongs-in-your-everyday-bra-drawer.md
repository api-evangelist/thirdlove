Key Takeaways A soft, stretchy lace bra in your true size can feel as comfortable as any everyday bra. A lace T-shirt bra, a plunge and a wireless bra or bralette together cover most outfits and necklines. Lace bralettes suit relaxed days, while a lined or structured lace bra gives a fuller bust more lift and shape.
