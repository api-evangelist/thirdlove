Key Takeaways Your breasts after breastfeeding typically get smaller, softer, and slightly less firm as milk production winds down and your body settles. Pregnancy hormones and skin stretching, not breastfeeding itself, are what cause breasts to soften and "relax." Most breasts find their new normal within roughly three to six months of weaning, though results vary from person to person. Supportive well-fitted bras, chest exercises, steady weight, and gradual weaning all help you feel firmer and more comfortable.
