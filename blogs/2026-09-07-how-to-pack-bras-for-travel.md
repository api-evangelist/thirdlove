Key Takeaways Pack bras last, nest them cup-in-cup, and stuff the cups with socks or underwear so they keep their shape. Fasten the hooks and tuck the straps into the top cup to prevent snags and tangles in your suitcase. Only fold soft-cup bras, since folding a molded or foam cup can leave a crease that shows under fitted tops.
