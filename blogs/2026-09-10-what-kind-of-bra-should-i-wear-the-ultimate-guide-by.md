Key Takeaways Pick your bra by matching three things: your neckline, your fabric, and how long you'll wear it. Under white or sheer tops, a nude-to-you shade with smooth molded cups hides better than a white bra. For weddings and formalwear, let the dress's neckline and back decide between a strapless, adhesive, or convertible bra.
