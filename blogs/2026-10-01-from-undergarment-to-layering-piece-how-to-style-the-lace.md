Key Takeaways A lace bralette works as a layering piece because its wire-free shape and visible lace look like a deliberate design detail. Match the bralette's neckline, lining, and color to how much of it you plan to show. Layer it under sheer tops, deep V-necks, and open knits, or wear it over a thin fitted shirt for a fashion-forward look.
