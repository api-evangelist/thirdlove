Key Takeaways Lace bras can feel comfortable every day when the lace is soft and the bra fits well. Itchy lace usually comes from stiff fibers, scratchy trim, tags, trapped sweat, or a fit that rubs. Look for stretch lace, tagless labels, a smooth lining, and a correct band and cup fit.
