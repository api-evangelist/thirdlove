Key Takeaways A firm, level band does most of the lifting, while the cups, center gore, side panels and straps finish the job. Match the style to your goal, such as push-up for cleavage, plunge for separation, full coverage for a heavy bust or minimizer for less projection. A wireless bra can give real lift when it uses structured knit, shaped cups and a firm band in place of soft stretch alone.
