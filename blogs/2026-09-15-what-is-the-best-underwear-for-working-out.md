Key Takeaways The best underwear for working out combines moisture-wicking stretch fabric, a breathable cotton gusset, and a cut that stays put. For sweaty sessions, choose nylon and spandex microfiber over all-cotton, and save cotton for low-impact days or sensitive skin. Match your style to your workout: a thong or bikini for leggings, a brief or hipster for high impact, and an anti-chafe short for runs and rides.
