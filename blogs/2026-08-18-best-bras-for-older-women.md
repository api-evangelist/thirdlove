Key Takeaways As your body changes with age, comfort, dependable support, and easy on-and-off wear become the top priorities in a bra. Prioritize wire-free support, wide straps and bands, soft breathable fabrics, and front closures with real adjustability. Thirdlove's best styles for older women include the 24/7 Classic T-Shirt Bra, wireless bras, front-closure bras, and full-coverage lift styles.
