Key Takeaways Match your bra to the outfit and neckline: smoothing T-shirt or wireless styles under fitted tees and chunky knits, plunge or demi under low-cut necklines, and supportive wireless styles under fitted ribbed tops. For strapless and off-the-shoulder looks, a convertible multiway bra with a band-first fit keeps everything in place with no straps showing. Backless dresses call for adhesive cups or a low-band convertible bra, chosen by how low the back dips.
